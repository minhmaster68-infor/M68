BÀI 6: EMERGENCY RESPONSE

I. Từ vựng khẩn cấp (Emergency Vocab)

Confined space: Không gian kín
Toxic gas: Khí độc
Unconscious: Bất tỉnh
Cease all hot work: Dừng mọi việc cắt hàn
Ventilation: Thông gió
SCBA: Bình khí nén cá nhân

II. Kịch bản cứu hộ (Rescue Dialogue)

Assistant: Mayday! Mayday! We have a welder unconscious in Hull 102 Engine Room!

Specialist: Copy that! Cease all hot work immediately! Is the space ventilated?

Assistant: Negative! Extraction fan is broken. Air is stagnant!

Specialist: Do not enter without SCBA! Wait for the rescue team!
